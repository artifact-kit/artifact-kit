# Commercial Licensing

Artifact Kit packages are available under a dual-license model:

- Open source license: AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Commercial license: required for proprietary use that does not comply with AGPL obligations.

The AGPL does not prohibit commercial activity by itself. It allows commercial
use, modification, distribution, and network use when the AGPL terms are
followed. Organizations that need closed-source usage, private modifications, or
terms outside the AGPL should obtain a commercial license from Artifact Kit.
